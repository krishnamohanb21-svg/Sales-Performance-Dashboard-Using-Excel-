# Sales-Performance-Dashboard-Using-Excel-
# Problem Statement: Sales Performance Dashboard Using Excel
A food and beverage manufacturer/distributor in North India sells 10 products (snacks, beverages, bakery, sauces, ready-to-eat) to 15 retail and wholesale customers across cities such as Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Rohtak and Chandigarh. In September 2026 the business recorded 450 invoices, about ₹18.9 lakh in net sales and ₹7.7 lakh in gross profit, plus a separate log of 60 operating expenses.

# The Problem
All of this data sits in raw transaction sheets (Sales_Data and Expense_Data). Management has no single view of how the business is performing. Without consolidated reporting, it is hard to answer basic questions:
Which products, categories, customers, cities and regions drive sales and profit?
How do daily and monthly sales trends move, and how much do discounts erode revenue?
How much revenue is at risk from Returned (78) and Pending (79) orders, which together are about 35% of all invoices?
How are customers paying (Cash, UPI, Credit, Bank Transfer), and what is the exposure to credit sales?
How do operating expenses by department and type, paid versus pending, compare with the gross profit earned?
Manual filtering and calculation of this data is slow, error-prone and not interactive.

# Objective
Build an interactive Excel sales dashboard that turns the raw sales and expense data into clear, decision-ready insights. It should use:
1) Data cleaning and preparation of Sales_Data and Expense_Data.
2) Calculated measures: Gross Sales, Net Sales, Discount %, COGS and Gross Profit.
3) Pivot tables and summary reports: daily and monthly sales, product-wise, customer-wise (with contribution %), city-wise and expense reports.
4)Visualizations: charts for Product, Category, Customer, City, Payment Mode and Region versus Sales, and Product versus Profit.
5) Slicers so users can filter the whole dashboard by region, category, order status and similar fields.

# Expected Outcome
A one-page dashboard showing key KPIs and trends at a glance.
Identification of top and bottom products, customers and regions.
Visibility into discount impact, returns, pending orders and unpaid expenses.
Faster, data-driven decisions on pricing, inventory, customer focus and cost control.

# Scope and Limitations
It covers one month (September 2026) of sales and expense data.
Returned and Pending orders are currently included in the Net Sales totals. A refined analysis should show them separately, or exclude them, for true realized revenue.
